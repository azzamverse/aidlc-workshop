# Delivery Planning Questions: Table Order v2

No questions were needed. The team's rules and the Unit split settle what this stage would ask:

- **What comes first:** the walking skeleton, U5, the smallest working slice that proves the pieces fit.
- **Bolt size:** one Unit per Bolt.
- **Parallel work:** Bolt 5 alone, then Bolts 6 and 7 in parallel.
- **Outside dependencies:** none beyond the free three.js library.

The biggest risk is the hand-in time, covered in `risk-and-sequencing-rationale.md`.
