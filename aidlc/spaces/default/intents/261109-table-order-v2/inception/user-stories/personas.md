# Personas: Table Order v2

Personas come from `requirements.md` and the users listed in the code notes (`business-overview.md`, `component-inventory.md`).

## Dana the Diner (primary)

- **Role:** a guest ordering from a phone at the table.
- **Goals:** order what is safe to eat, make dishes her own way, know when food is coming, and have fun at the table.
- **Pain points:** waiting without knowing how long, catching a waiter's eye, and worrying about allergens.
- **Tech comfort:** medium.
- **Frequency:** once per visit.

## Kofi the Kitchen Lead

- **Role:** runs the kitchen board at the pass.
- **Goals:** see new orders at once, read exactly what each diner asked for, and keep staff pages private.
- **Pain points:** missed tickets, and special requests lost on the way to the kitchen.
- **Tech comfort:** medium.
- **Frequency:** all service.

## Farah the Floor Staff

- **Role:** looks after tables, answers calls and closes bills.
- **Goals:** see which table needs her and clear the request once she has been there.
- **Pain points:** diners waving for attention, and no way of knowing who is waiting.
- **Tech comfort:** medium.
- **Frequency:** all service.

## Priority and Relationships

Dana comes first, because every differentiating feature is hers. Kofi and Farah share one staff PIN and the same board, and they respond to what Dana asks for.
