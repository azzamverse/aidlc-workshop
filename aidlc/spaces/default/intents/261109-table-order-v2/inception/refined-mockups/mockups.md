## Kept From v1

The 3-tab bottom bar, sticky category pills, "View cart" and "Place order" above the tabs, image on the left of each card, and the "+ Add" pill at the far right of the price row.

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- Dish names and prices are examples, and the seed data is fixed in Domain Design.
- The mockups show one state each. The other states are defined in `interaction-spec.md`.
