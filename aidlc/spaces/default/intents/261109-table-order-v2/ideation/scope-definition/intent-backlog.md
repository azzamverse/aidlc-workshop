# Intent Backlog: Table Order v2

All six features are Must Have, because together they are the minimum the brief requires. They come from `intent-statement.md` and are confirmed in `scope-document.md`. The order below is by dependency first, then by value to the demo.

| Rank | Capability | MoSCoW | Depends on | Reason for its place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shared foundation and design system | Must Have | v1 | Everything new shares it |
| 2 | Staff PIN | Must Have | Foundation | It changes how every staff page opens, so it goes in early |
| 3 | Make it yours | Must Have | Foundation | It changes what an order holds, which later features display |
| 4 | Allergy shield | Must Have | Foundation | Protecting diners is the strongest reason to choose this app |
| 5 | Animated order tracker | Must Have | Foundation | It is the most visible part of the diner's wait |
| 6 | Notifications | Must Have | Foundation | It completes the wait, and alerts staff to new orders |
| 7 | Call the waiter / Ask for the bill | Must Have | Foundation | It closes the diner's visit and stands out in the demo |

## Won't Have This Time

QR codes, Multi-language, Menu management, Past visits and sales, Shared table cart, Kitchen stations, Live floor map, Chef's special drop, Honest wait time, Kitchen ticket timers, "Same again", Manager TV wall, Dark mode, and Rate your meal.

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- The ranking is a suggested order. How these are grouped into Units and Bolts is decided in Units Generation and Delivery Planning.
