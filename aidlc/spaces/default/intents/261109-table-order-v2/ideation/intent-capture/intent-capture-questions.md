B. Make it yours: items have options such as spice level, no onions, extra cheese or size, which change the price and show on the kitchen ticket (Recommended)
C. Shared table cart: several phones at one table share one live cart, showing who added what
D. Kitchen stations: each item belongs to a station with its own board, and an order is Completed when every station has finished
E. Live floor map: staff see every table coloured by what is happening there, updated live
F. Chef's special drop: staff launch a limited special with a countdown and a stock count, live on every open menu
G. Honest wait time: the diner sees an estimated wait based on today's orders and how busy the kitchen is
