# Personas: Table Order

Two personas, from the two user groups in the intent statement and `requirements.md`.

## P1. The Diner

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | Seated at a table, using the customer view on their own phone |
| Goals | Order at their own pace; see how each order is progressing; change their mind before the kitchen starts |
| Pain points | Waiting for staff to take the order; not knowing whether it is being made |
| Context | One-handed on a phone, with others at the table who may order from their own phones |
| Tech comfort | Low to high: the app must need no explanation |
| Frequency | Once per visit, with no sign-in |

## P2. The Member of Staff

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | Floor or kitchen staff, sharing the one staff view on a larger screen |
| Goals | See every table and new order at a glance; move orders through the kitchen; bill and reset a table quickly |
| Pain points | Losing track of orders; no single live view of all the tables |
| Context | Busy, on a shared screen on the local machine, with interruptions |
| Tech comfort | Medium |
| Frequency | Throughout service |

## Relationships and Priority

Both personas are primary, because the journey needs both. The diner starts each order and the member of staff finishes it. Each sees the effect of the other's actions live.

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- Floor and kitchen staff are one persona because they share one view (Intent Capture Q2).
