### Summary

The component split is sound and acyclic, and its references resolve. Two Major gaps should be fixed at the gate. One is how a cart learns and checks its visit, which FR10.5 and FR10.7 depend on. The other is who owns transactions and the placement-against-close race. The Minor items are small contract clarifications.
